# Privacy Policy for Downloader

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Downloader is a macOS application for downloading files, torrent content, and links sent from Safari. This Privacy Policy explains how Downloader handles information.

## Data Collection

Downloader does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Downloader does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Local Data

Downloader may store download-related information locally on your device, including:

- Download URLs
- File names and file paths
- Download progress and status
- Torrent metadata needed to manage selected torrent files
- App preferences and Safari Extension settings

This information is stored locally to provide the app's download management features. It is not sent to the developer.

## Safari Extension

The Downloader Safari Extension may read webpage links and download-related URLs when you use the extension or when automatic download capture is enabled. This is used only to send eligible download links to the Downloader app.

The extension may use shared local storage through the app's App Group to pass pending download links from Safari to the main app.

## Network Access

Downloader connects to the internet to download files, retrieve torrent metadata, and participate in torrent transfers when you choose to download torrent content.

Network requests are made directly from your device to the servers, peers, or trackers required for the downloads you start.

## Torrent Downloads

When using torrent features, torrent metadata, selected file information, peer connections, tracker communication, download progress, and upload activity may be processed locally by the app as part of normal torrent operation.

Downloader does not send this information to the developer.

## Data Sharing

Downloader does not share personal information with third parties.

Downloaded content, download URLs, torrent activity, and app preferences remain on your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

## Data Deletion

You can delete download records and downloaded files using the app's controls. You can also remove local app data by deleting the app and its related local containers from your Mac.

## Children's Privacy

Downloader is not designed to collect information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.
